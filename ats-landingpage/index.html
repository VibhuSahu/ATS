About

A clear look at how your resume matches a job.

What is this project?

ATS Resume Analyzer is a web app that scores how well a resume matches a job description. You paste the job description, upload your resume as a PDF, and get back an ATS match score. It runs on a FastAPI backend with a scikit-learn model.

Why was it developed?

Most applicants send a resume without ever knowing how an automated screening system will read it. This project was built to open up that black box: a simple, free way to check a resume against a real job description before you apply.

The problem

What problem does it solve?

Applicants submit resumes into automated screening and hear nothing back. They can't see what the system looked for, so they can't fix what is missing. ATS gives that feedback before you hit submit.

Existing problems and limitations

  • Applicant tracking systems filter resumes automatically, and applicants get no view of the score.
  • Rejections come with no explanation, or never come at all.
  • How a resume is scored stays hidden inside recruiter software.

Challenges faced by users

  • Not knowing which parts of a job description really matter.
  • Rewriting a resume by guesswork for every single application.
  • No quick way to compare a resume with a specific role.

Why existing solutions fall short

  • Generic resume tips ignore the job description in front of you.
  • Some checkers put results behind sign-ups or payment.
  • Closed tools give no way to see or improve how the score is made.

How ATS addresses these challenges

Built around your target role

Your resume is scored against the exact job description you paste, not a generic checklist.

One page, one upload, one score

No account and no setup. Add the two inputs and read the result.

Open for anyone to inspect

The code, model and deployment setup are public under the MIT license.

Why ATS

What makes it different?

Matched to the job

Free and open source

How it works

From resume to score in four steps.

1

Paste the job description

Copy the posting you are applying to into the text box.

2

Upload your PDF resume

Choose your resume file and press Analyze Resume.

3

The API analyzes both texts

The FastAPI backend reads the PDF and compares it with the job description using the trained model and vectorizer.

4

Get your ATS match score

See how well your resume fits, then adjust it before you apply.
